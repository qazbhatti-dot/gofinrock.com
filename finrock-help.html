Getting started & plans

Is Finrock really free?+

Yes — every account starts free with no card: all 14 My Life records, renewals, documents and reminders, net worth, spending and goals, statement import and ten receipt scans a month. You pay when you connect a live bank feed (Individual, £9.99/mo) or have a trade, a let or a company to run — Sole trader £12.99, Self-employed and Landlord £24.99, Limited company £39.99 per company, all including VAT. Add a qualified accountant who checks every return from £10/mo more.

What happens to my data if I cancel?+

Your account drops to the Free plan rather than locking. Records, documents and history stay readable and exportable as CSV. Deleting the account removes the data entirely.

Does it work on my phone and my laptop?+

Both. The mobile app is built for the daily habit — capture a receipt, sort a few items, check a renewal. The desktop view is built for the longer sessions: the full ledger, reconciliation, reports and filing.

Banking & receipts

How do I connect my bank account?+

On any paid plan (Individual £9.99 and up): Settings → Accounts → Add account. You authorise read-only access at your own bank, which takes about a minute, and transactions start arriving within a few minutes. Finrock pulls up to 12 months of history on first connection. On the Free plan, import a CSV, OFX or QIF statement instead — everything else works the same.

How does receipt capture work?+

Point the camera at the receipt — at the till is fine. Finrock reads the supplier, date, amount and VAT, matches it to the bank transaction when one exists, and stores the image with the entry. Paper can go in the bin; HMRC accepts digital copies.

How does sorting work?+

Anything needing a decision lands in Sort — one item per screen with a suggested answer already filled in. You confirm business or personal, the category, and the VAT treatment if it matters. Most items are one tap; rules you set are applied automatically next time.

Tax, VAT & filing

Does Finrock actually file with HMRC?+

Not yet. Finrock prepares your Self Assessment, quarterly updates for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax and VAT returns from your books, ready to file. Filing to HMRC from the app is planned — for now, submit your Self Assessment on HMRC's website, and send quarterly updates and VAT returns through your accountant or other software that can file them. Then mark it filed in Finrock so the record stays in one place.

What does the Filing & deadlines page show?+

Every regime that applies to you — VAT, Self Assessment, MTD quarterly updates, Corporation Tax, CIS — with its next deadline, what’s ready, and what’s missing. Regimes that don’t apply to you are hidden.

Where do my documents live?+

In the vault: receipts, contracts, certificates, ID and anything you attach to a record. Everything is encrypted, searchable, and linked to the thing it belongs to — the MOT certificate sits on the vehicle, the lease on the property.

Can my own accountant use it?+

Yes, free, on any plan. Invite them from Settings and they get scoped access to the books you choose, with a full access log. You can withdraw it in one tap. If you’d rather we provide the accountant, that’s the accountant-checked plans.

Invoicing & getting paid

How do invoices and quotes work?+

Create a quote or invoice from a contact in under a minute, send it with a payment link, and Finrock tracks sent, viewed, paid and overdue. Quotes convert to invoices in one tap, and overdue chasing reminders can go out automatically.

Can it handle my bills and subscriptions?+

Yes. Bills get due dates and reminders, recurring ones are detected from the bank feed, and the Bills view shows what’s leaving your account before it does — so nothing bounces or surprises you.

Does Finrock do payroll?+

On Limited company, yes — payroll, payslips and P60s for yourself as a director or a small team, from the same place as the rest of the books. The FPS figures are prepared for your payroll filer or accountant to send.

Mileage, CIS & payroll

Can I track business mileage?+

Yes — log a trip in seconds or add it after the fact. Finrock applies the approved rates (45p for the first 10,000 miles, 25p after) and puts the claim into your books and your tax workings automatically.

I’m a CIS subcontractor — does Finrock cover that?+

Yes. Finrock tracks CIS deductions on your income, keeps the statements with the entries, and includes the reclaim in your Self Assessment workings.

What reports can I get?+

Profit & loss, VAT position, spending by category, cash flow forecast, budgets and year-end summaries — all live from the ledger, all exportable. Your accountant sees the same numbers you do.

Ltd companies & year-end

Can I use it for a limited company as well as sole trade?+

Yes. A limited company gets its own workspace and its own books, kept properly separate from your sole trade and your personal money. You can switch between them, and payroll and dividend handling are available on the Limited company plan.

How does year-end work?+

Finrock walks the checklist: unsorted items, missing receipts, draft invoices, then locks the year and produces the figures for the return. For a limited company it prepares the year-end close your accountant signs off.

Documents & proof

Can I get proof of income for a mortgage or letting agent?+

Yes — Finrock generates a proof-of-income statement from your filed figures, ready to hand to a lender, letting agent or broker.
