Questions

I’m a sole trader and a landlord. Do I pay twice?

No. You’re one taxpayer, so it’s one plan. Every trade and every property sits on the same subscription — £14.99 under the MTD threshold, £24.99 inside it.

Which plan do I need?

Not self-employed: My Life. Self-employed or letting with qualifying income under £50,000 (£30,000 from April 2027): Sole trader & Landlord. Over it: the MTD plan. A limited company: £49.99 per company, with your personal plan included.

Does Finrock file to HMRC?

Finrock prepares your Self Assessment, quarterly updates and VAT return from your books, ready to file. Direct submission switches on when HMRC recognition lands — until then you file the figures in one step.

Why does a limited company include an accountant?

Company accounts and a CT600 carry director liability. Every Limited company plan has a qualified UK accountant review and sign off the year end — it’s in the £49.99.

How is Finrock different from other accounting apps?

Other apps see your business account. Finrock sees business and personal together, so it can help you and your accountant spot income that might otherwise be missed — before HMRC does. Flagging for large or business-looking personal payments is coming soon.

Can I start without a card?

Yes. Start free — renewals, documents, net worth and statement import. Paid plans come with a 7-day free trial and cancel any time.
