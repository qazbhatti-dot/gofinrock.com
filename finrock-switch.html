Be straight

When not to switch.

You need direct filing with HMRC today, with no accountant
Finrock prepares every return, ready to file. Direct submission switches on when HMRC recognition lands. Until then you or your accountant send it, or you add an accountant for £10 a month and they file for you. If that doesn’t suit, stay where you are for now.
Your software is free with your bank account
Keep it for the books. Use Finrock’s My Life at £9.99 for what it doesn’t cover: the budget, the bills, the renewals and the life admin.
You have staff, stock or several people in the accounts
The bigger packages are built for that. Finrock is for one person’s whole financial life: sole traders, landlords and owner-run companies.
How long does switching take?
Most people are set up in an evening. Connect your bank, or upload last year’s statements, and Finrock reads every line. Reply to any email and a person helps.
Is it safe to put everything in one place?
Bank access is read-only: Finrock can see transactions and can never move money. Data is held in the UK, encrypted, never sold, and you can export or delete it at any time.
