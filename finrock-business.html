Questions

Who is our accountant?

On Limited company + accountant, a qualified UK accountant (ACA, ACCA or CIMA) on the Finrock team, assigned to your company. You see their name and can message them in the app.

Can I keep my own accountant?

Yes — that’s the £39.99 plan. Give them free access to the accountant portal; they see your books live and do the year end themselves.

I have two companies.

Each company is its own set of books and its own filing, so a second company is another company at the same price. Your My Life workspace is included once, whatever you have.

I have a company and a buy-to-let.

Your own lets are Sole trader & Landlord, not part of the company plan: £9.99 off beside a company, so £44.99 in all with your own accountant, £54.99 with ours. Inside MTD the lets plan is £15 instead of £24.99.

Does Finrock file to Companies House and HMRC?

Accounts and the CT600 are prepared from your books, ready to file. Sending from the app switches on when recognition lands; until then your own accountant, or ours on Limited company + accountant, files them.
