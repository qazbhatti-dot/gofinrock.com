Questions

Who is the accountant?

A qualified UK accountant (ACA, ACCA or CIMA) on the Finrock team, assigned to your company. You see their name and can message them in the app.

Can I keep my own accountant?

Yes. Give them free access to the accountant portal — they see your books live and can do the year end themselves. The plan price is the same.

I have two companies.

Each company is its own set of books and its own filing, so it’s £49.99 per company. Your personal plan is included once, whatever you have.

I have a company and a buy-to-let.

The company plan includes your personal Sole trader & Landlord plan, so the let is covered. If your personal trade and rent together are over the MTD threshold, it’s +£10 for quarterly updates.

Does Finrock file to Companies House and HMRC?

Accounts and the CT600 are prepared and signed off, ready to file. Direct submission switches on when recognition lands; until then your accountant files them for you.
