How we got here

A short history.

2019
The shoebox problem

Two of our founders were running a UK practice. The same January, the same reconstructed year, the same clients who had done nothing wrong except be busy.

2022
MTD stopped being optional

Making Tax Digital pushed quarterly reporting towards every sole trader and landlord. Records kept once a year stopped working — the software had to keep them continuously.

2024
One ledger, both sides

We stopped treating business and personal as two products. Same ledger, each entry marked as you sort it, and the personal side grew into My Life.

2026
One life. One app.

Sole trader, landlord, director or none of the above: one app, one price per person, and a qualified accountant a tap away.
