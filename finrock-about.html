Why we exist

The software split a life that isn't split.

Accounting tools were built for accountants, and money apps were built for spending. Neither knew what your MOT was due, or that the £40 at the builders' merchant was for a rental property, or that your dividend income changes what you owe in January.

Meanwhile a sole trader was paying for bookkeeping software, a receipt app, an accountant, and still doing the sorting themselves at midnight. The pieces existed. Nothing joined them up.

Finrock is one ledger. Business and personal in the same place, each entry marked as you sort it, and the filings — VAT, MTD, Self Assessment — falling out of the records rather than being rebuilt from scratch every year.

What we hold to

Four rules we don't bend.

Show the working

Every figure links back to the entries behind it. No number appears that you cannot open, question and correct.

One decision at a time

Sorting is a swipe with an answer already suggested — not a spreadsheet of dropdowns at eleven at night.

Read-only, always

Bank access is read-only Open Banking. Finrock cannot move your money, and never sees your bank login.

Honest prices

Prices include VAT because most sole traders cannot reclaim it. £24.99 means £24.99 leaves your account.

How we got here

A short history

2019

The shoebox problem

Two of our founders were running a UK practice. The same January, the same reconstructed year, the same clients who had done nothing wrong except be busy.

2022

MTD stopped being optional

Making Tax Digital pushed quarterly reporting toward every sole trader. Records kept once a year stopped working — the software had to keep them continuously.

2024

One ledger, both sides

We stopped treating business and personal as separate products. A single ledger with a business/personal marker turned out to make Self Assessment far simpler, not harder.

2025

Filing, built and tested

VAT and Making Tax Digital for Income Tax submission built and tested against HMRC’s test service. Live filing from the app is planned.

2026

My Life, free forever

The personal side — property, pension, vehicle, insurance, documents — became a free plan in its own right. It stays free.
