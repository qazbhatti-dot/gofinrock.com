Questions

I’m a sole trader and a landlord. Do I pay twice?

No. You’re one taxpayer, so it’s one plan — £14.99 under the MTD threshold, £24.99 inside it. Every trade and every property is included.

I only have rental income. Is this for me?

Yes. The plan is named for both because HMRC files both on the same return. You get the SA105 property pages, jointly-owned lets split for you, and the gas safety, EICR and insurance renewals remembered alongside.

I also have a limited company.

Your company has its own plan at £49.99 — and it includes your personal Sole trader & Landlord plan. See director pricing.

Does Finrock file to HMRC?

Finrock prepares every return from your books, ready to file. Direct submission switches on the day HMRC recognition lands.

What about CIS?

Deductions are tracked per contractor and per statement, and land in the right box on your Self Assessment.
