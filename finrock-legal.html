What we collect, why, and how long we keep it

Privacy notice

Who we are

Finrock Ltd, registered in England & Wales, is the data controller for the information you give us when you use Finrock. Reach the privacy team at privacy@gofinrock.com.

What we collect

  • Account details — name, email, password hash and two-factor settings.
  • Financial records — transactions you import or that arrive through a read-only bank connection, plus invoices, receipts and the categories you assign.
  • My Life records — the property, pension, vehicle, insurance, health, family and document entries you choose to create.
  • Tax information — the figures needed to calculate and prepare VAT, Making Tax Digital and Self Assessment returns, and responses HMRC sends back.
  • Technical data — IP address, device and browser type, and event logs needed to keep the service secure.

Why we process it

To provide the service you asked for (performance of our contract); to meet legal duties around tax record keeping; to keep the service secure and detect abuse (legitimate interests); and, where you have opted in, to send product updates. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we never sell it.

Bank connections

Bank data arrives through a regulated Open Banking provider. You authenticate at your own bank; your credentials never pass through Finrock. Access is read-only and can be revoked from your bank or from Settings at any time.

How long we keep it

For as long as you use Finrock — HMRC generally expects business records to be kept for at least five years after the filing deadline. When you delete your account, personal data is removed, save the minimum we must retain to evidence submissions made on your instruction.

Who we share it with

Only the processors needed to run the service — hosting, Open Banking, email, payments, error monitoring — each under a data processing agreement; HMRC when you instruct us to file; and, on accountant-checked plans, the accountant who reviews your return. A current subprocessor list is available on request.

Where it is held

Under UK jurisdiction on UK infrastructure. Where a processor operates outside the UK, transfers rely on UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement.

Three kinds, and no advertising trackers

Cookies

Strictly necessary

Keep you signed in, remember two-factor state, protect forms against cross-site request forgery, hold your workspace selection. Cannot be switched off without breaking sign-in.

Preferences

Theme, default view, last-used columns. Clearing them only resets those choices.

Analytics

Aggregated, privacy-preserving usage measurement so we can see which parts of the app confuse people. Not tied to your financial data; decline it in Settings → Privacy without losing anything.

What you can ask for, and how fast we answer

Your UK GDPR rights

Your rights

  • Access — Settings → Export gives you your full ledger, life records and documents.
  • Rectification — every record is editable; figures link to the entries behind them.
  • Erasure — delete your account in one tap, subject only to records we must keep to evidence filings made on your instruction.
  • Restriction — ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved.
  • Portability — machine-readable CSV.
  • Objection — to processing based on legitimate interests, including product messaging.
  • Withdraw consent — disconnect a feed, revoke HMRC authorisation or turn off analytics at any time.

For anything not available in the app, email privacy@gofinrock.com; we respond within one month, usually far sooner.

Complaints

Tell us first — we would rather fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Automated decisions

Finrock suggests categories and tax treatments automatically, but nothing is applied without your confirmation, and no decision with a legal effect on you is made by automation alone.

The agreement between you and Finrock Ltd

Terms of service

The service

Finrock provides bookkeeping, record keeping, tax preparation and life-admin software for UK individuals, sole traders, landlords and small limited companies. By creating an account you agree to these terms. You must be 18 or over and resident in the UK.

Your responsibilities

The records in your account are yours, and so are the returns prepared from them. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter or import, for reviewing figures before filing, and for keeping your credentials and two-factor device secure. Finrock is a tool that helps you get it right; it does not become the taxpayer.

Plans, prices and billing

Every account starts free and requires no card. Paid plans, inclusive of VAT and billed monthly in advance: My Life £9.99; Sole trader & Landlord £14.99 (self-employment and/or rental income under the MTD threshold); Sole trader & Landlord · MTD £24.99 (inside MTD — every trade and every property on one plan); Limited company £49.99 per company, including qualified accountant sign-off of the year end and the director’s personal Sole trader & Landlord plan (if the director is personally inside MTD, quarterly updates are +£10, £59.99 in total). Accountant check +£10 on either personal plan. Paid plans start with a 7-day free trial; cancel at any time and access continues to the end of the paid period. We will never raise the price of a plan you are on.

Cancelling

One tap from Settings. Access continues to the end of the paid period, after which the account reverts to free rather than locking. Export everything before or after.

Filing and professional advice

Returns are prepared from your records and released only after you have reviewed and approved the figures. Any submission to HMRC is made under your own Government Gateway authorisation. Guidance in the app is general information, not tailored advice. Where a plan includes an accountant’s review, that review is an additional check, not an assumption of your filing obligations.

Availability

We aim to keep Finrock available at all times and publish status openly, but do not warrant uninterrupted service. Planned maintenance avoids filing-deadline weeks.

Liability

Nothing here limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or anything that cannot lawfully be limited. Subject to that, our total liability in any twelve-month period is limited to the fees you paid in that period; we are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for penalties arising from figures entered incorrectly or filings approved without review.

Ending the agreement

Close your account at any time. We may suspend or end an account for non-payment, unlawful use or abuse — with notice and an opportunity to export, except where the law requires otherwise.

Governing law

The law of England & Wales; its courts have exclusive jurisdiction.

For accountants and practices using the portal

Data processing agreement

The relationship

Where a practice uses Finrock to hold or review client records, the practice is normally the controller and Finrock a processor acting on its instructions. Our standard DPA is available at privacy@gofinrock.com — signed electronically, no negotiation required for standard terms.

What it covers

  • Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing; categories of data and data subjects
  • Processing only on documented instructions; confidentiality
  • Security measures: encryption in transit and at rest, access control, audit logging
  • Subprocessor list, notice of changes, right to object
  • Assistance with data subject requests, breach notification without undue delay, audit rights
  • Deletion or return of data at the end of the engagement

Client-granted access

Where a client invites you into their own account rather than joining your portal, the client remains the controller and grants you scoped, revocable, logged access, withdrawable at any time.
