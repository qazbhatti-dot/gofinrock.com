Pricing questions

I’m a sole trader and a landlord. Do I pay twice?

No. You’re one taxpayer, so it’s one plan. Every trade and every property sits on the same subscription — £14.99 under the MTD threshold, £24.99 inside it.

What counts towards the MTD threshold?

Self-employment turnover plus gross rent, before expenses. Over £50,000 for 2026/27, £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028. Salary, pension and dividends don’t count.

What happens when HMRC brings me into MTD?

Switch from Sole trader & Landlord to the MTD plan in one tap. Your books, receipts and history carry straight over — nothing to re-enter.

Does the company plan cover my own trade or lets?

Your company plan includes your My Life workspace and your own Self Assessment for salary and dividends, prepared from your books, ready to file. It no longer includes your own trade or lets: those are Sole trader & Landlord at £9.99 off, with our accountant +£10 as for anyone.

Does a limited company need an accountant?

A company return needs an accountant or filing software — HMRC’s free company filing service closed on 31 March 2026. Limited company is £39.99 when your own accountant files. Limited company + accountant is £49.99: ours checks every return against your books, queries anything odd, and files it for you wherever the rules let an accountant — and tells you when a return needs your own confirmation.

I have a limited company and rental income. What do I pay?

£44.99 if your own accountant files the company, £54.99 with our accountant: the company plan plus Sole trader & Landlord at £9.99 off. If your trade and rent together are inside MTD, the trade plan is £14.99 instead of £9.99.

Do I get a discount for having more than one thing?

You never pay per income: every trade and every property is one plan, and My Life is free with any other plan. A director who also has their own trade or lets adds Sole trader & Landlord at £9.99 off. A second company is another company at the same price — separate accounts, a separate CT600.

Why do your prices include VAT?

Because most sole traders can’t reclaim it. A “£19 + VAT” plan really costs £22.80. £24.99 means £24.99 leaves your account.

Is it tax-deductible?

For the self-employed, yes — software for the business is an allowable expense. At basic rate with Class 4 NI, £24.99 costs you about £18.50.

Will the price go up?

We will never send you a price-rise email. The price you join on is the price you keep.

What does our accountant do?

A qualified UK accountant checks every return against your books, queries anything odd, and files it for you wherever the rules let an accountant — and tells you when a return needs your own confirmation. It’s +£10 on either Sole trader & Landlord plan; on a company it’s Limited company + accountant.

Does Finrock file to HMRC?

Finrock prepares every return from your books, ready to file. Sending from the app switches on the day HMRC recognition lands — until then your own accountant, our accountant or you send the prepared figures.
