Pricing questions

I’m a sole trader and a landlord. Do I pay twice?

No. You’re one taxpayer, so it’s one plan. Every trade and every property sits on the same subscription — £14.99 under the MTD threshold, £24.99 inside it.

What counts towards the MTD threshold?

Self-employment turnover plus gross rent, before expenses. Over £50,000 for 2026/27, £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028. Salary, pension and dividends don’t count.

What happens when HMRC brings me into MTD?

Switch from Sole trader to Self-employed in one tap. Your books, receipts and history carry straight over — nothing to re-enter.

Does the company plan cover my personal Self Assessment too?

Your personal plan is included, so your Self Assessment is prepared from your books, ready to file. The accountant sign-off in the company plan covers the company — accounts and CT600. If you want the accountant to check and submit your personal return as well, that’s the usual +£10 add-on.

Why does the Limited company plan include an accountant?

Company accounts and the CT600 carry director liability, so every company’s year end is reviewed and signed off by a qualified UK accountant. It’s in the £49.99, per company.

I have a limited company and rental income. What do I pay?

£49.99. The company plan includes your personal Sole trader & Landlord plan, so the let is covered. If your personal trade and rent together are inside MTD, quarterly updates are +£10 — £59.99 in all.

Do I get a discount for having more than one thing?

You never pay per income: every trade and every property is one plan. Directors get their personal plan included with the company. The only thing charged more than once is a second company — it’s a separate set of accounts and a separate CT600.

Why do your prices include VAT?

Because most sole traders can’t reclaim it. A “£19 + VAT” plan really costs £22.80. £24.99 means £24.99 leaves your account.

Is it tax-deductible?

For the self-employed, yes — software for the business is an allowable expense. At basic rate with Class 4 NI, £24.99 costs you about £18.50.

Will the price go up?

We will never send you a price-rise email. The price you join on is the price you keep.

What does the +£10 accountant check include?

A qualified UK accountant reviews your Self Assessment, quarterly updates and VAT return against your books, queries anything odd, then submits them to HMRC for you through their own agent account. You approve; they file. Available on Sole trader and Self-employed plans; included in Limited company.

Does Finrock file to HMRC?

Finrock prepares every return from your books, ready to file. Direct submission switches on the day HMRC recognition lands — until then you file the prepared figures in one step.
