Pricing questions

What does our accountant do, and does Finrock file to HMRC?

A qualified UK accountant checks every return against your books, queries anything odd, and files it for you wherever the rules let an accountant — and tells you when a return needs your own confirmation. They start when you subscribe, not during the trial. Have your own accountant? Switch ours off and invite yours in. Finrock itself prepares every return from your books, ready to file. Sending from the app switches on the day HMRC recognition lands — until then your own accountant, our accountant or you send the prepared figures. A company return needs an accountant or filing software: HMRC’s free company filing service closed on 31 March 2026.

Am I in MTD, and what changes if I am?

It’s decided by self-employment turnover plus gross rent, before expenses: over £50,000 for 2026/27, £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028. Salary, pension and dividends don’t count. Inside MTD the plan also prepares your quarterly updates and VAT return, at £24.99 a month, or £34.99 with our accountant. Moving up is one tap and your books, receipts and history carry straight over. Check where you stand in 2 minutes.

Is My Life really free, and what is Plus?

Yes, for good, no card. Free My Life covers your bills and subscriptions, renewals and reminders, your documents vault, one connected bank account and 10 questions a month to the AI assistant. My Life Plus, at £4.99 a month, connects every account and makes the AI assistant unlimited, and adds receipt scans, net worth and the tax view for PAYE, savings and dividends. Plus is included free with every other plan.

What’s in the price?

VAT is included, because most sole traders can’t reclaim it. For the self-employed the subscription is an allowable business expense; what that saves you depends on your own tax position. We will never send you a price-rise email. After the trial an unpaid account can still read its books, and you can cancel any time.
