Questions

Is any of it free?

Yes. Renewals, documents, net worth and statement import are free with no card. £9.99 adds live bank feeds, unlimited scanning and every bill in one view.

I have a small side income. Which plan?

Under the £1,000 trading allowance you need nothing extra — My Life covers it. Above it, Sole trader & Landlord prepares your Self Assessment.

Can Finrock move my money?

No. Access is read-only through regulated Open Banking. Finrock can see; it can never pay, transfer or cancel.

Where are my documents kept?

Encrypted in the UK, in transit and at rest. They’re yours — export everything any time.
