One page per thing

A property is one page. Not five.

What it is — value, mortgage, fix-end — and what it did — rents, costs, the Section 24 position — together, with the paperwork attached. The same for your pension, your car, your investments.

Every figure links back. Tap a number anywhere and see the entries behind it. Documents live with the record. Deeds with the property, policy with the insurance. CGT handled. The investments register tracks cost basis, so a disposal's gain is right.
