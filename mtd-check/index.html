Questions

Does Finrock file to HMRC?

Finrock prepares every return, ready to file. Your 2025-26 return you send on HMRC’s website. Quarterly updates are sent by your accountant, or by ours with the £10 accountant check. Direct sending from Finrock switches on when HMRC recognition lands.

I own my lets jointly.

Only your share of the rent counts, for the threshold and on your return. Finrock splits joint lets for you.

Do I need a separate update for each property?

No. All your UK lets are one property business, so one update a quarter (overseas lets are another).

What if my income drops?

If it falls below the threshold for three years in a row, you can leave MTD.

Can my accountant see my books?

Yes. Invite them; the practice portal is free for them.
