The controls

What protects your account.

Read-only bank access

You authorise at your own bank; Finrock never sees your login. No payments, ever. Revoke from your banking app any time, or skip feeds and import statements.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

TLS on every connection; encryption at rest for records and documents. The vault is stored separately from ledger data.

Two-factor, required

Authenticator app with SMS fallback. Sessions expire; see and revoke devices in Settings.

HMRC through your own gateway

Filing uses your Government Gateway authorisation, granted and revocable by you. Every submission logged both ways.

Accountant access you control

Scoped, logged, read-only access to the books you choose. Withdraw in one tap; the log shows everything opened.

Export and erasure

Full ledger, documents and life records as CSV and files, any time. Deletion removes your data — no win-back holds.

In plain terms

What Finrock does — and never does.

Reads transactions to suggest a category — you confirm. Stores documents against the record they belong to. Prepares returns under your own HMRC authorisation. Lets you export or delete everything without asking us.

Questions

Where is my data held?

Under UK jurisdiction on UK infrastructure. Where a processor operates outside the UK, transfers rely on UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement.

Who can see my books?

You. An accountant only if you invite them, to the books you choose, logged. Finrock staff only with your permission for a support case, and it’s logged.

How do I report a vulnerability?

Email security@gofinrock.com. We acknowledge within 24 hours and don’t pursue good-faith researchers.

Where are the legal documents?

All on one page: terms, privacy, cookies and the DPA.
