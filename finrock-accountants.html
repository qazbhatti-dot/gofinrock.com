The quarterly rhythm

MTD makes it quarterly. The portal makes it manageable.

01

The queue finds the work

A practice-wide work queue surfaces which client needs what, by when — quarters due, year-ends waiting, receipts missing. No spreadsheet of deadlines.

02

Review on real books

Drill from any figure to the entries behind it. Query a transaction with a message pinned to the entry itself — the client answers in the app.

03

Approve, sign off, filed

Quarterly updates reviewed and approved, year-end signed off, and a filing log both sides so 'what did HMRC receive?' always has an answer.
