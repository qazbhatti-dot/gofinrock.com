How it works

The client does the sorting. You do the accounting.

01 · Client

They sort from the van, in a tap.

Bank feed in, business or personal suggested, category picked, receipt matched. Your client keeps the ledger current in minutes a week — you never chase a shoebox again.

  • Merchant memory pre-answers most rows
  • Receipts attached to the transaction
  • Trade and property on one ledger

02 · Query

Ask them in the app, not by email.

Flag a row, the client gets a notification, answers from their phone, the note sits on the transaction forever. No spreadsheet of questions, no lost thread.

  • Queries on the transaction itself
  • Client answers in plain English
  • Audit trail kept

03 · Review

Quarterly updates and year ends, queued.

Every client’s quarterly update, VAT return and Self Assessment lands in your review queue when it’s ready, with the ledger behind every figure. Approve, adjust, or send it back.

  • One queue across every client
  • Every figure traces to its transactions
  • Deadlines and thresholds tracked for you

04 · Integrity · coming soon

See what other apps never show you.

Most accounting apps only see the business account. Finrock sees business and personal accounts in one ledger, so it can flag income that may have been missed before you sign anything off. Large or business-looking payments marked personal need a reason from the client, and the evidence sits on the transaction.

  • Large personal credits flagged for a reason
  • Platform and customer payments marked personal, flagged
  • Gift letters and loan terms uploaded in-app
  • Client confirms all income before you file

Questions

Is the portal really free?

Yes. It’s paid for by your clients’ plans. No seat fees, no client minimum.

Can I move a client’s existing books in?

Import opening balances and prior-year figures by CSV; bank history back-fills from the feed.

Can my client see what I see?

You see their ledger; they see your notes and queries. Your working papers stay private to the practice.

Will I see income a client marked as personal?

That’s the plan. Because Finrock sees personal accounts as well as business ones, large or business-looking credits marked personal will be flagged, need a reason from the client, and appear in your review. It helps you spot what’s missing; it can’t see accounts the client hasn’t connected, so the client still confirms their income is complete before you file. Coming soon.

Does Finrock file to HMRC?

Returns are prepared, ready to file; you can file from your own agent account today. Direct submission switches on when recognition lands.
