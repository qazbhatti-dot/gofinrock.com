The rules where your property is

England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland have different rules. Finrock knows which apply.

Each let is held to its own nation’s law: the kind of tenancy, the deposit deadline, the certificates and registration it needs. Finrock suggests the nation from the postcode, and you can change it. England’s changes since 1 May 2026 are included.

England

Tenancy type, deposit protection, gas safety, EICR, EPC and the changes from 1 May 2026.

Wales

Occupation contracts, Rent Smart Wales registration and the certificates each let needs.

Scotland

Private residential tenancies, landlord registration, deposit deadline and safety checks.

Northern Ireland

Landlord registration, deposit scheme and the certificates the let needs.

The law itself is on GOV.UK: Renting out your property. Finrock keeps the dates; it isn’t legal advice.

Questions

I only have rental income. Is this for me?

Yes. The plan’s named for both because HMRC files trading and property on the same return.

My letting agent takes fees out of the rent.

Record the gross rent and the fees as a cost; Finrock does it from the agent’s statement or from your bank.

Do I need an accountant?

Not to use Finrock. If you have one, invite them free. If you’d like one, add our accountant check for £10 a month.

Does Finrock file to HMRC?

It prepares every return; you or your accountant send them. Direct sending switches on when HMRC recognition lands.

Is my data safe?
