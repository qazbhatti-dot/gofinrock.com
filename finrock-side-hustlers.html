FAQ

Questions we get from side-hustlers.

I’m under £30k from side income. Do I still need MTD?

Not yet — but you still need to file Self Assessment if your side income is over £1,000 (the trading allowance). Start free to see your figures; move to Sole trader (£12.99) for the tracking and a Self Assessment prepared, ready to file.

Can I deduct my streaming subscription?

No. Mark a personal subscription as personal once and Finrock remembers it next time. What is genuinely deductible — a share of your phone bill, mileage to client meetings, pro-rated home-office costs — gets booked to the business side.

I’m a landlord with one flat. Is that self-employment?

No — it is property income, a separate category for tax, and it goes on the SA105. But it counts the same way for MTD thresholds. Finrock treats it identically: categorise, attribute to the property, and the SA105 is ready to file.

What if I have a job, a side hustle and a rental?

That is the full set, and it is the case Finrock was built for. PAYE is already with HMRC; the side hustle and the rental need declaring, and from £30k combined MTD-compatible software becomes mandatory.

I missed a few years of Self Assessment. Can you help?

Yes. Finrock can bring earlier years into your books, and your accountant can use them to make a voluntary disclosure to HMRC — considerably less stressful than HMRC finding it first.
